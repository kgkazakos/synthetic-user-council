# Example Output: Enterprise HR Performance Review Platform

**Research Question:** Why do managers skip the standardised performance review templates and write their own freeform reviews instead?

**Researcher's planned question:** "What do you find limiting about the current review format?"

---

## Council Summary

Veteran managers intentionally withhold structured data to protect top performers from forced calibration algorithms. Mandatory evaluation criteria alienate internal teams by exclusively measuring client-facing metrics. Newly promoted managers use plain text to avoid making a technical mistake that ruins employee compensation.

---

## Blind Spots Identified

1. The impact of the backend calibration algorithm — managers actively starve it of structured data to prevent their teams from being mathematically downgraded in forced distribution curves.
2. Deep-seated trust issues regarding how HR uses standardised metrics for forced rankings or layoffs — this goes far beyond usability concerns.
3. The false assumption that the entire firm consists of billable client-facing staff — internal operations teams cannot be meaningfully evaluated using client-facing metrics.
4. Severe anxiety among newly promoted managers who inherit direct reports — they default to freeform out of fear of making a technical mistake that damages compensation, not out of preference.

---

## Challenged Assumptions

1. Managers bypass templates because they want more nuance — many bypass them to maintain control over career trajectories and avoid automated scoring.
2. Skipping the template saves time — some managers spend significantly more time writing extensive offline evaluations to circumvent the system.
3. Non-compliance is always conscious rebellion — for newly promoted managers, it is often a panic response to an unnavigable interface.

---

## Suggested Questions for Your Real User Study

1. Tell me about your understanding of how the data in these performance reviews is used by HR after you hit submit.
2. Walk me through how you evaluate direct reports who do not fit the standard client-facing billable profile.
3. Think back to the last time you inherited a team member from another manager. How did you handle their historical goals in the system?
4. Describe the exact moment in your last review cycle where you decided to switch to a freeform document instead of the template.
5. Tell me about a time you felt the platform's standardised scoring might negatively impact one of your high performers.
6. Walk me through any offline tracking systems or spreadsheets you use to track team performance outside the platform.

---

## Segments to Prioritise

1. **Veteran team leads with highly profitable pods** — to investigate strategic avoidance of the calibration algorithm and offline tracking habits.
2. **Managers of non-billable internal operations teams** — to explore how required metrics fail their staff.
3. **Newly promoted managers who recently inherited teams** — to understand usability failures and anxiety around goal transitions.
