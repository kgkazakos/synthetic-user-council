# Example Output: Consumer Fintech Identity Verification

**Research Question:** Why do users start but never complete the identity verification process?

**Researcher's planned question:** "What made you uncomfortable about the verification process?"

---

## Council Summary

Biometric demands occur before the app proves its core value to new users. Intimidating compliance language makes vulnerable users fear legal trouble for simple camera errors. Technical barriers and social context physically prevent willing participants from completing the flow.

---

## Blind Spots Identified

1. Ignoring the physical and social context — many users are prompted to take a selfie while on public transit, where performing an exaggerated, well-lit face scan feels socially impossible.
2. Overlooking fear of legal or financial repercussions from intimidating compliance language — first-time users interpret UX warnings as legal threats.
3. Failing to recognise hardware limitations — strict capture algorithms are physically impossible for users with tremors or low-quality front cameras.
4. Missing the value-exchange equation — users are asked for biometric data before the app has demonstrated any value or established trust.

---

## Challenged Assumptions

1. 'Uncomfortable' means fearful for data privacy — in reality, it means socially awkward in public or terrified of making a legal mistake.
2. Drop-offs are conscious choices — many users are physically locked out by strict algorithms or hardware incompatibility.
3. Users trust a faceless digital tool enough to hand over biometric data before the product has established legitimacy.

---

## Suggested Questions for Your Real User Study

1. Tell me about where you were and what you were doing the last time you opened a financial app to make a quick payment.
2. Walk me through what went through your mind when you first saw the instructions for the photo verification.
3. Describe a time you abandoned a sign-up process because it asked for too much information upfront.
4. If you could explore the app's dashboard before verifying your identity, how would that change your willingness to submit a photo?
5. Tell me about a time an app or website rejected a photo or document you tried to upload.
6. How did you interpret the specific warnings and language used on the camera screen?

---

## Segments to Prioritise

1. **Users with older devices or physical mobility limitations** — to test the accessibility of facial recognition algorithms.
2. **People who rely on immediate access to wages** — to understand how fear of mistakes impacts completion rates.
3. **Commuters and users on the go** — to observe the social friction of taking selfies in public.
